# Resolve reference qualities separately from identity and allocate duration by samples

Reference sources supply per-property evidence and retain hash/span/alignment
lineage. Target voice/speaker IDs remain owned by the Script. Equal-priority
sources conflict until a primary is chosen; unvalidated blending, emotion,
intonation and shout/whisper conditioning remain explicit unsupported outcomes.
Feature measurement never means the requested delivery has been rendered.

Duration is a budget at its own clip/segment/sentence scope. Parent totals include
internal pauses; explicit sentence durations remain fixed and the remaining
samples are distributed among free units. A duration lock pins that unit's budget;
it does not assign the whole clip duration to each descendant. Style locks retain
the existing ancestor cascade. Generic effective settings are descriptive intent;
only the duration planner may turn scoped duration into per-sentence targets.

Plans and feature caches are project-owned. Source content/alignment and target
text/identity/settings signatures detect stale plans. An unchanged unit does not
invalidate merely because another binding changed. Explicit timing plans win;
a whole-sentence Script binding can otherwise supply its primary timing envelope.
Output/subsentence bindings remain precise Fix/Patch targets, and are not silently
expanded to whole-sentence generation. Historic training footage requires the
run's hashed manifest and a retained matching PCM segment, beyond a selection tick.

OmniVoice native duration runs with silence postprocessing disabled and padding0;
product-owned reconciliation permits at most60ms token-frame quantization padding
or removal of literal zero samples. It never cuts audible samples to meet a
number, and rejects clipping, silence and infeasible budgets. Final sample counts
must meet complete clip/segment budgets before publication. Natural generation
retains its existing behavior. Upstream engines remain read-only. Duration/fit
policy and timing source keys participate in clip identity; model/prompt revision
fingerprints continue to protect warm runtime state.

Duration matching is distinct from word-anchor matching, intelligibility, and
voice identity acceptance. Actual audio is published with measured duration and
needs-listening status; estimated words never certify anchors. Explicit navigation
is separate from expansion/selection and playback follow. Native browser and
human listening gates remain separate from component/API and PCM evidence.
