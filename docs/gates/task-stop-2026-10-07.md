# TASK-STOP-01 — uniform two-level Stop

Date: 2026-10-07. Implementation gate; hardware acceptance remains open.

## Delivered

- Shared Stop button and in-app Yes/No dialog for STT, Speaker Diarization,
  Training and Generate Voice. First click stops the queue; two clicks within
  1.2 seconds offer confirmed active cancellation. No retains queue Stop.
- General STT includes growing MKV and OBS detailed STT. A recording-specific
  OBS control supports Stop and explicit rerun without stopping PCM capture.
- Task-owned cancellation fences, bounded acknowledgement, preserved completed
  output/checkpoints, training Resume ownership and generation intake reservation.
- Warm Studio children are reused on ordinary files; only owned cancellation,
  engine-kind switching, release or idle expiry retires them.
