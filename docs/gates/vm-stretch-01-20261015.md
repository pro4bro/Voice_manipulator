## Results

FFmpeg reported the `atempo` audio filter. The adapter rendered all five runs
with exact 49,920-frame output. Processing times were 125, 187, 79, 78, and
62 ms; nearest-rank p95 (n=5) was 187 ms, within the frozen 250 ms target. All
runs reported 200 zero-padding frames (8.33 ms), zero clipped samples, and a
maximum boundary step of 0.00638 full scale. The source SHA-256 before and after
remained `1c1555fc49eee3b21155538358867ac89d48f2a904fb3b487f8ee42d5b3079a8`.
All five output WAVs have the same SHA-256
`a5fffc8c9c19ee44b9b8a3c457c7c0ae8e87f12a7365c6f37dd91d19d8436a92`; each
retained the untouched prefix and suffix PCM byte-for-byte. Artifacts are in
`.scratch/vm-rounds11-15/vm-stretch-01/`.

The focused adapter tests pass, including a 440 Hz synthetic tone held within
the frozen ±2% pitch tolerance, byte-exact untouched PCM, sequential current-
timeline operations, silence handling, and rejection of clipping/click
boundaries or audible overrun. This does not test browser playback while dragging,
late-render cancellation, human listening quality, multi-speaker speech, or
reopen/undo integration. The short actual-speech fixture is not a p95 population;
the five-run p95 is only a local smoke measurement.
